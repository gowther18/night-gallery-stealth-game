NIGHT GALLERY is a top-down stealth game: you are Wren, a cat burglar inside the Marlowe Gallery after closing, and you must steal the Moon Opal from its central plinth and slip out through the skylight exit unseen. Four night guards patrol the wings and the central hall with flashlight vision cones that are clipped by walls and cover; display cases, planters and benches hide you, dark floor patches slow detection, and the lit red carpet is fast but exposed. Sneak with Shift, toss pebbles with Space or a click to lure guards away, and mind the detection eye — get caught three times and the night watch wins.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.
